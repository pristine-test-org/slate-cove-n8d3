How Borage works

Borage is the calm front door to the shared list: it shows the latest items from the list on a single page, so you can see where things stand without opening anything else.

Three quiet steps

  1. Open the front door and read the short overview at the top.
  2. Scroll to the live section to see the five newest items.
  3. Finished items carry a check mark so you can skim past them.

The list refreshes every time the page loads, so there is nothing to sync.
