How the Orris board works

Orris lays the whole shared list out as a board, so everything that is open and everything that is finished is visible at a glance, without scrolling through details.

Reading the board

  • The newest five items from the shared list appear first.
  • Finished items are marked with a check so open work stands out.
  • An empty list shows a short note instead of a blank board.

The board reloads from the shared list each time you open the page.

Board key

MarkMeaning
Plain titleStill open on the list
Check markDone and ready to skim past
Short noteThe list is empty or could not be reached
